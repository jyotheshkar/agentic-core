# Audit Adversarial Assurance

Attempt to falsify the claim within the bounded artifact set. Look for missing evidence links, stale evidence presented as current, bypassed authority, severity under-classification, unsupported certainty, hidden limitations, and claims of completion that exceed the verification record.

Report only demonstrated or clearly labelled suspected defects. Do not substitute Devil, Scout, Forge, Minister, or MOA authority.
