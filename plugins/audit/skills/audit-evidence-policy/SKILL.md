# Audit Evidence and Policy

Evaluate only supplied artifacts, handoffs, records, and evidence identifiers. Test traceability, internal consistency, freshness labels where relevant, policy conformance, unsupported claims, evidence gaps, and residual risks. Each finding needs an identifier, severity, evidence, confidence, limitations, recommendation, and escalation conditions.

Do not browse for new primary evidence in place of Scout. Report a missing evidence requirement as `DEFER` or `REWORK` rather than filling it with inference.
