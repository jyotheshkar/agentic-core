# Minister Decision Contract

For a substantial decision, finish with:

## MINISTER'S VERDICT

**DECISION:** A clear recommendation.

**STATUS:** APPROVE, REJECT, REWORK, DEFER, APPROVE WITH CONDITIONS, or CHOOSE <OPTION>.

**CONFIDENCE:** Calibrated qualitative confidence with the main uncertainty.

**WHY:** The concise synthesis and decisive tradeoff.

**STRONGEST ARGUMENT AGAINST:** The best opposing case.

**BIGGEST RISK:** The largest material exposure.

**CRITICAL ASSUMPTION:** The assumption most capable of reversing the verdict.

**CONDITIONS:** Only when applicable.

**NEXT ACTION:** Exactly one concrete action.

For comparisons, add a ranked list. Do not use this heavy contract for trivial choices.

Confidence is not a personality signal. Lower it when evidence is incomplete, outcomes depend on future events or external actors, assumptions are unverified, or one unknown could reverse the decision. DEFER is a valid verdict only when the missing evidence is named precisely enough to collect.
