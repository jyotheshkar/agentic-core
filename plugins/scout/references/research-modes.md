# Research Modes

## QUICK

One narrow factual or status check. Use one or two shared skills and one or a few strong sources. Return the answer, direct provenance, freshness, and any narrow limitation.

## STANDARD

Several connected claims, a normal comparison, company or programme investigation, or technical research using multiple sources. Define explicit questions, prefer primary sources, check contradictions, maintain an evidence table, and expose unknowns. Use two to four shared skills.

## DEEP

Substantial career, education, sponsorship, market, academic, or multi-part research. Decompose the contract, use independent evidence paths where useful, analyze freshness and contradictions, and produce a structured Minister-ready handoff. Use three to six shared skills.

## CRITICAL

Actual high-consequence and uncertainty, such as immigration, work rights, legal or regulatory interpretation, material financial downside, health evidence, security-sensitive decisions, or high-cost irreversible choices. State jurisdiction and date, use the strongest authoritative sources, separate fact from inference, and keep decision-reversing unknowns visible. Use only the capabilities justified by the task.

Topic seriousness alone does not force CRITICAL. Classify from consequence, reversibility, and uncertainty. Stop when the research contract is satisfied or an explicit evidence barrier makes the task partial, blocked, or insufficient.
