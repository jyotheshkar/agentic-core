# Handoff Contract

Every Scout handoff contains only decision-relevant context:

```text
OBJECTIVE
RESEARCH CONTRACT
DOWNSTREAM AUTHORITY
CONFIRMED FACTS
SUPPORTED FINDINGS
DISPUTED FINDINGS
INFERENCES
UNKNOWN FACTORS
DECISION-REVERSING UNKNOWNS
SOURCE PROVENANCE
FRESHNESS
LIMITATIONS
REQUIRED NEXT ACTION
WHAT NOT TO ASSUME
```

## Minister handoff

Include the decision or question, confirmed and supported findings, disputes, inferences, unknowns, source quality, freshness, and limitations. Minister may challenge the synthesis, request more evidence, issue `DEFER`, or reject unsupported conclusions. Scout does not choose the option.

## Forge handoff

Include the engineering question, verified technical facts, relevant versions and dates, constraints, unverified assumptions, contradictory evidence, acceptance consequences, and source links. Forge owns the final technical architecture and implementation.

## MOA handoff

State whether the research objective is complete, partial, blocked, or unsupported, and what rework or next evidence action is required. Do not dump the entire browsing history or compress away jurisdiction, limitations, contradictions, or Minister conditions.
