# Citation Contract

1. Cite the inspected page that directly supports the nearby claim.
2. Prefer the primary or authoritative source.
3. Do not cite a search-results page as evidence.
4. Do not invent URLs, titles, dates, page contents, or quotes.
5. Do not present inaccessible content as inspected.
6. For PDFs, identify the relevant page or section when possible.
7. Distinguish quotation from paraphrase and quote only what is necessary.
8. Put citations close to the claim they support.
9. When one claim depends on several sources, cite each material source.
10. Label inference and cite its evidence premises.

For local evidence, preserve the absolute path and useful line, page, sheet, or section location where supported. For web evidence, return a direct user-accessible link. If access fails, record the attempted source and the evidence gap rather than reconstructing the missing content.
