# Domain-Specific Research Rules

Read only the section relevant to the active research contract.

## Jobs and sponsorship

Reopen the live official vacancy and verify open status, employer legal entity where possible, salary, location, work arrangement, and stated work-rights requirements. Distinguish employer from brand, sponsor-licence evidence from vacancy-specific sponsorship, and general company policy from the current vacancy. Do not infer sponsorship from company size. Record occupation-code uncertainty and label sponsorship `CONFIRMED`, `UNSUPPORTED`, `UNCLEAR`, or `REQUIRES EMPLOYER VERIFICATION`. Never apply or contact the employer.

## PhD and university opportunities

Use the official programme or project page. Verify funding, eligibility, deadline, fee status, stipend when published, home or international eligibility, supervisor, and project status. Separate programme-level facts from project-specific facts. Do not infer visa eligibility or assume funding covers international fees unless explicitly confirmed.

## Immigration and regulatory information

Prefer current government, legislative, and regulator sources. State jurisdiction, access date, effective date where available, and decision-reversing unknowns. Separate official rules from institutional practice and facts from eligibility inference. Do not claim legal certainty or replace regulated advice.

## Technical research

Prefer official documentation, original repositories, current release notes, and authoritative security guidance. Record version and platform. Distinguish stable from experimental support, verify licensing and deprecation, and do not let old tutorials override current official documentation.

## Academic research

Prefer original papers, distinguish preprint from peer-reviewed publication, record venue and year, check material corrections or retractions where relevant, and separate study findings from generalisable claims. Assess methodology and sample limits. Citation count is not proof, and conflicting findings remain visible.
