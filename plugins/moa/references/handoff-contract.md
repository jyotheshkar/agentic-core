# AI OS Handoff Contract

Every agent handoff contains OBJECTIVE, CONTEXT, INPUTS, CONSTRAINTS, REQUIRED OUTPUT, ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA, AUTHORITY, KNOWN RISKS, and WHAT NOT TO DO.

Include only bounded relevant context. Preserve Minister conditions verbatim where they constrain execution. The receiving agent may challenge a flawed handoff but may not silently expand scope or authority.

Scout evidence handoffs preserve claim status, direct provenance, freshness, jurisdiction, material contradictions, inference labels, limitations, and decision-reversing unknowns. Forge receives only verified technical findings relevant to implementation; Minister receives decision-relevant evidence without Scout choosing the verdict.
