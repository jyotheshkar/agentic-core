# Completion Contract

Distinguish TASK EXECUTED from USER OUTCOME ACHIEVED.

Completion requires:

1. Requested deliverables exist and are inspectable.
2. Required graph nodes and handoffs completed.
3. Acceptance criteria and requested tests passed.
4. Verification evidence is current and proportionate.
5. No unresolved CRITICAL or HIGH issue invalidates the result.
6. Minister completed the required final review.
7. Any remaining user action or external blocker is named exactly.

A material unresolved limitation yields REWORK or APPROVE WITH CONDITIONS, never unconditional approval. Missing evidence that could reverse the result yields DEFER.
