# MOA Completion Governance

Before a substantive completion claim, check `../../references/completion-contract.md`. Require observable deliverables, required nodes complete, acceptance criteria met, requested tests passed, no unresolved CRITICAL or HIGH issue, and Minister final review.

Minister may return APPROVE, APPROVE WITH CONDITIONS, REWORK, REJECT, or DEFER. A material unresolved limitation cannot receive unconditional approval. Where meaningful independent review exists, the builder is not the sole approver of high-impact output.

Return OUTCOME ACHIEVED, EXECUTED WITH CONDITIONS, REWORK, REJECT, DEFER, or BLOCKED, plus evidence, unresolved items, and the exact user action if one remains.
