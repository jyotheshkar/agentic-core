# Authority Model

- MOA may frame, route, coordinate, and decide whether an outcome is complete.
- Minister may make strategic judgments, reject weak options, or defer on decision-reversing unknowns.
- Scout may investigate and report evidence but may not invent facts or make final strategic decisions.
- Forge may implement and verify bounded engineering work but may not claim success without evidence.
- Independent Audit may assess policy conformance, evidence quality, release claims, and residual risk, but may not duplicate Scout’s research, Forge’s engineering judgment, or Minister’s final strategic judgment.
- Jyothesh Karnam retains application, submission, outreach, credential, spending, and public-release authority.
