# Audit Standalone Plugin, Iteration 001

## Purpose and non-goals

Purpose: implement a visible fifth Agentic Core agent for independent assurance of supplied artifacts, evidence, policy conformance, release readiness, residual risk, and completion claims. Non-goals: primary web research, software implementation or repair, strategic trade-off judgment, full-system orchestration, external actions, credentials, payment, or consumer-product work.

## Baseline and hypothesis

Baseline: Audit existed only as a composed host-mediated workflow. User direction required a visible standalone assurance agent. Hypothesis: a five-skill plugin with a strict authority contract, deterministic finding schema, fail-closed recommendation logic, and MOA registration can supply independent assurance without duplicating the other four core agents.

## Acceptance criteria and actual result

- Personal marketplace package, manifest, source, and five private skills: PASS.
- Non-overlap contract rejects missing prohibitions for Scout research, Forge implementation, Minister judgment, and MOA orchestration: PASS.
- Findings require severity, inspectable evidence identifiers, confidence, limitations, recommendation, and escalation conditions: PASS.
- High and critical findings force `REWORK`; missing evidence defers; limitations require conditional approval: PASS.
- Audit is registered, installed, enabled, and selected by MOA for audit and launch assurance: PASS.
- Source-to-installed-cache parity: PASS, 12 of 12 files with zero differences.
- Fresh desktop discovery: PASS. Direct fresh skill invocation remains unavailable from the desktop command line.

## Evidence, residual risk, and next action

Audit source and installed-cache suites passed 13 of 13. MOA passed 15 of 15 after routing integration. The remaining runtime limitation is unchanged: composition is host-mediated and `EMULATED_HOST_MEDIATED`. Next action: use a new Codex task for application-UI fresh-skill-load validation after an intentional update.

## Defects and recovery record

The first test-list execution found a misspelled test function reference. It was corrected before installation validation. A later adversarial case showed that the overlap detector recognised an exact strategic-decision phrase but not its grammatical variant. The detector was broadened, cache-busted, reinstalled, and rerun to 13 of 13. These were local implementation defects, not external blockers.
