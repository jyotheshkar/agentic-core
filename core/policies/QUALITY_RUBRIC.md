# Shared Skill Quality Rubric v0.1

Score each applicable dimension from 0 to 5: 0 absent, 1 poor, 2 weak, 3 acceptable, 4 strong, 5 excellent. Mark a dimension not applicable only with a written reason; do not award automatic points.

1. Purpose clarity
2. Trigger quality
3. Domain depth
4. Workflow specificity
5. Boundary clarity
6. Evidence and source quality
7. Progressive disclosure
8. Context efficiency
9. Reusability
10. Testability
11. Failure handling
12. Compatibility
13. Maintainability
14. Duplication and overlap control
15. Security considerations

Maximum score is 75. Recommended states: below 45 EXPERIMENTAL, 45 to 59 review required, 60 to 75 eligible for VERIFIED after all blocking validation passes. A high score cannot override a structural, security, provenance, or behavioral failure.
