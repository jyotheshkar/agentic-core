# Risk Assessment Method

## Rating bands

- Likelihood: rare, unlikely, plausible, likely, near-certain.
- Impact: limited, material, severe, existential for the defined objective.
- Detectability: early and clear, observable with monitoring, late or ambiguous, effectively hidden until impact.
- Recoverability: easy, manageable, difficult, irreversible.

Use numbers only when supported by observed frequencies, credible base rates, contracts, or measured system behavior.

## Severity

- CRITICAL: threatens the core objective or creates unacceptable irreversible harm and needs immediate design change.
- HIGH: could materially defeat the plan and needs a named control before commitment.
- MEDIUM: material but manageable through planned mitigation or contingency.
- LOW: limited effect that can be accepted or monitored.

## Dependency analysis

Identify shared causes, cascading failures, external approvals, timing chains, key-person dependencies, funding assumptions, and single vendors or systems. Several individually moderate risks may form one high correlated exposure.

## Control quality

Rank controls by eliminate, reduce, transfer, contain, detect, and accept. A control needs an owner, timing, mechanism, and trigger. Record residual risk after the control.

## Stop conditions

Define evidence that should pause, redesign, or terminate the plan before sunk cost and escalation make exit harder.
