# Model Evaluation

## Workflow

1. Translate use-case success and harms into measurable evaluation questions.
2. Choose baselines, datasets, slices, metrics, thresholds, and uncertainty treatment before testing.
3. Evaluate normal, boundary, adversarial, shifted, and abstention behavior proportionately.
4. Report aggregate and slice results, limitations, reproducibility, and decision implications.

## Output contract

An evaluation plan or report with baselines, slices, thresholds, uncertainty, and limitations.

State evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, and missing capability separately when they affect the result. Stop or defer when a decision-reversing unknown cannot be resolved safely.

## Boundary

Do not claim general capability from a narrow benchmark or aggregate score.

This skill is advisory expertise. MOA owns orchestration, Minister owns strategic judgment, Forge owns engineering implementation, and verification owns completion evidence.

## Evidence basis

Use the relevant parts of the [primary guidance](https://airc.nist.gov/) when standards or formal evidence are material. Verify currency before making a time-sensitive claim.
