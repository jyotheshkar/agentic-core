# Responsible AI Safety

## Workflow

1. Map the use context, affected people, actors, intended benefits, foreseeable misuse, and failure impact.
2. Assess validity, reliability, safety, security, privacy, transparency, bias, and human oversight.
3. Choose measurable controls, owners, escalation paths, stop conditions, and residual-risk acceptance authority.
4. Monitor deployed behavior and revisit controls when models, data, users, or context change.

## Output contract

An AI risk profile with evidence, controls, ownership, monitoring, and residual risk.

State evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, and missing capability separately when they affect the result. Stop or defer when a decision-reversing unknown cannot be resolved safely.

## Boundary

Do not equate a framework checklist with proof that an AI system is safe, fair, or compliant.

This skill is advisory expertise. MOA owns orchestration, Minister owns strategic judgment, Forge owns engineering implementation, and verification owns completion evidence.

## Evidence basis

Use the relevant parts of the [primary guidance](https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-ai-rmf-10) when standards or formal evidence are material. Verify currency before making a time-sensitive claim.
