# Quality Assurance

## Workflow

1. Define quality characteristics, acceptance evidence, risk tolerance, and decision authority.
2. Place prevention, review, automated checks, and independent verification at the cheapest effective points.
3. Track defects by impact, cause, escape point, owner, and verified correction.
4. Use trends and retrospectives to improve the system rather than only inspect outputs.

## Output contract

A quality plan with gates, evidence, defect handling, ownership, and improvement loop.

State evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, and missing capability separately when they affect the result. Stop or defer when a decision-reversing unknown cannot be resolved safely.

## Boundary

Quality assurance cannot certify unknown work or replace domain-specific verification and user acceptance.

This skill is advisory expertise. MOA owns orchestration, Minister owns strategic judgment, Forge owns engineering implementation, and verification owns completion evidence.

## Evidence basis

Use the relevant parts of the [primary guidance](https://committee.iso.org/quality-management/principles) when standards or formal evidence are material. Verify currency before making a time-sensitive claim.
