# Systems Analysis Method

## Building the model

Choose a boundary and time horizon that include the mechanism behind the outcome. Name excluded factors that could reverse the conclusion. Distinguish stocks that accumulate, flows that change them, constraints that limit throughput, and information delays that distort decisions.

## Feedback

- Reinforcing loop: change compounds in the same direction.
- Balancing loop: change triggers a counterforce toward a target or limit.
- Delay: causes action to arrive after decision-makers expect it, often producing oscillation or overcorrection.

Do not label a loop without tracing a closed causal path. Separate evidence-backed relationships from hypotheses.

## Leverage

Consider parameters, buffers, information flows, rules, incentives, goals, and the system boundary. Higher leverage is not automatically safer. Prefer reversible interventions with early signals when the model is uncertain.

Check whether improving one component shifts cost, delay, risk, or workload elsewhere. Monitor both the target metric and likely displacement metrics.
