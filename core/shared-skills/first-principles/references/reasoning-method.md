# Reasoning Method

## Constraint audit

- Fundamental: physical, logical, legal, or mathematically necessary.
- Empirical: observed and testable, but potentially revisable.
- Chosen: a user or product requirement that remains binding unless scope changes.
- Conventional: common practice that may encode useful experience but is not necessary.
- Estimated: approximate and sensitive to inputs.
- Assumed: treated as true without adequate evidence.

For each challenged constraint, state the evidence, risk of being wrong, and cheapest discriminating test. Prefer a small reversible test over a grand redesign when uncertainty is high.

## Reconstruction questions

What outcome must exist? What functions are necessary? What can be removed? What can be combined? What is the smallest experiment? Which constraint dominates? What alternative framing makes the problem easier without changing the objective?

Stop decomposing when further detail does not change a decision or implementation.
