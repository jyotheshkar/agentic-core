# Project Delivery

## Workflow

1. Define the outcome, acceptance evidence, scope, constraints, and decision authority.
2. Map work, dependencies, owners, critical path, and safe parallelism.
3. Set evidence gates, risk controls, review cadence, and change rules.
4. Track achieved outcomes and blockers, not activity volume alone.

## Output contract

A dependency-aware delivery plan with owners, gates, risks, and status rules.

State evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, and missing capability separately when they affect the result. Stop or defer when a decision-reversing unknown cannot be resolved safely.

## Boundary

A delivery plan does not authorize spending, external communication, or irreversible action.

This skill is advisory expertise. MOA owns orchestration, Minister owns strategic judgment, Forge owns engineering implementation, and verification owns completion evidence.

## Evidence basis

Use the relevant parts of the [primary guidance](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/agile-delivery) when standards or formal evidence are material. Verify currency before making a time-sensitive claim.
