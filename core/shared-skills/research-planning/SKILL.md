# Research Planning

## Workflow

1. Translate the decision or deliverable into answerable research questions.
2. Define source classes, inclusion and exclusion criteria, freshness needs, and evidence hierarchy.
3. Create search tracks, a capture schema, contradiction handling, and a stopping rule before collecting broadly.
4. Record unresolved questions and the evidence that could change the conclusion.

## Output contract

A research protocol with questions, criteria, search tracks, evidence fields, and stopping rule.

State evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, and missing capability separately when they affect the result. Stop or defer when a decision-reversing unknown cannot be resolved safely.

## Boundary

Do not imply that PRISMA reporting makes an ordinary investigation a systematic review.

This skill is advisory expertise. MOA owns orchestration, Minister owns strategic judgment, Forge owns engineering implementation, and verification owns completion evidence.

## Evidence basis

Use the relevant parts of the [primary guidance](https://www.prisma-statement.org/prisma-2020) when standards or formal evidence are material. Verify currency before making a time-sensitive claim.
