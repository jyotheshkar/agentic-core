# Requirements Engineering

## Workflow

1. Identify stakeholders, intended outcomes, operating context, and authority.
2. Separate functional requirements, quality attributes, constraints, assumptions, and exclusions.
3. Write observable acceptance criteria and resolve contradictions by decision authority.
4. Maintain traceability from objective to requirement to test and change decision.

## Output contract

A requirements set with priorities, acceptance criteria, dependencies, exclusions, and traceability.

State evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, and missing capability separately when they affect the result. Stop or defer when a decision-reversing unknown cannot be resolved safely.

## Boundary

Do not convert preferences or guessed future needs into mandatory requirements without evidence.

This skill is advisory expertise. MOA owns orchestration, Minister owns strategic judgment, Forge owns engineering implementation, and verification owns completion evidence.

## Evidence basis

Use the relevant parts of the [primary guidance](https://insights.sei.cmu.edu/library/software-requirements/) when standards or formal evidence are material. Verify currency before making a time-sensitive claim.
