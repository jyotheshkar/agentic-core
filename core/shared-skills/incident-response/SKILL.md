# Incident Response

## Workflow

1. Establish incident authority, severity, affected assets, timeline, and safety constraints.
2. Preserve evidence while containing harm and preventing uncontrolled changes.
3. Recover through verified steps with monitoring and rollback, then communicate facts and uncertainty.
4. Run a blameless learning review and track corrective actions to evidence-based closure.

## Output contract

An incident record with decisions, timeline, containment, recovery evidence, and follow-up actions.

State evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, and missing capability separately when they affect the result. Stop or defer when a decision-reversing unknown cannot be resolved safely.

## Boundary

Do not destroy evidence, expose sensitive details, or make consequential external statements without authority.

This skill is advisory expertise. MOA owns orchestration, Minister owns strategic judgment, Forge owns engineering implementation, and verification owns completion evidence.

## Evidence basis

Use the relevant parts of the [primary guidance](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/61/r3/final) when standards or formal evidence are material. Verify currency before making a time-sensitive claim.
