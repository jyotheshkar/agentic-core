# Product Discovery

## Workflow

1. Define the affected users, current behavior, unmet outcome, and existing alternatives.
2. Separate problem evidence from solution enthusiasm and list the riskiest assumptions.
3. Design the cheapest ethical tests for value, usability, feasibility, and viability.
4. Recommend build, narrow experiment, further discovery, or stop with explicit evidence thresholds.

## Output contract

A discovery brief with problem evidence, assumptions, tests, and a go, test, or stop recommendation.

State evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, and missing capability separately when they affect the result. Stop or defer when a decision-reversing unknown cannot be resolved safely.

## Boundary

Discovery reduces uncertainty. It does not guarantee demand or authorize implementation.

This skill is advisory expertise. MOA owns orchestration, Minister owns strategic judgment, Forge owns engineering implementation, and verification owns completion evidence.

## Evidence basis

Use the relevant parts of the [primary guidance](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/agile-delivery/how-the-discovery-phase-works) when standards or formal evidence are material. Verify currency before making a time-sensitive claim.
