# Experience Design

## Workflow

1. Map the user goal, entry points, decisions, dependencies, and failure states.
2. Choose an information structure and interaction sequence that minimizes unnecessary cognitive load.
3. Specify empty, loading, error, permission, confirmation, and recovery states.
4. Check keyboard access, semantics, contrast, target size, reflow, and non-visual equivalents.

## Output contract

A flow and state specification with accessibility and recovery behavior.

State evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, and missing capability separately when they affect the result. Stop or defer when a decision-reversing unknown cannot be resolved safely.

## Boundary

Do not claim WCAG conformance without testing the implemented product against applicable success criteria.

This skill is advisory expertise. MOA owns orchestration, Minister owns strategic judgment, Forge owns engineering implementation, and verification owns completion evidence.

## Evidence basis

Use the relevant parts of the [primary guidance](https://www.w3.org/TR/wcag/) when standards or formal evidence are material. Verify currency before making a time-sensitive claim.
