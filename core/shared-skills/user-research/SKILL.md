# User Research

## Workflow

1. State the decision the research must inform and what is currently unknown.
2. Choose participants and methods that can answer that question without unnecessary data collection.
3. Use consent, privacy, accessibility, and safeguarding appropriate to the context.
4. Separate observed behavior, participant report, researcher interpretation, and product implication.

## Output contract

A research plan or findings brief with method, sample limits, evidence, and implications.

State evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, and missing capability separately when they affect the result. Stop or defer when a decision-reversing unknown cannot be resolved safely.

## Boundary

Do not diagnose participants, expose sensitive data, or treat a small convenience sample as population truth.

This skill is advisory expertise. MOA owns orchestration, Minister owns strategic judgment, Forge owns engineering implementation, and verification owns completion evidence.

## Evidence basis

Use the relevant parts of the [primary guidance](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research) when standards or formal evidence are material. Verify currency before making a time-sensitive claim.
