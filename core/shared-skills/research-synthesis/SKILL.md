# Research Synthesis

## Workflow

1. Group evidence by question and claim rather than by source order.
2. Weight evidence by directness, quality, currency, and applicability.
3. Explain contradictions and heterogeneity instead of forcing consensus.
4. State the supported conclusion, confidence, limitations, and evidence gaps that could change it.

## Output contract

A source-traceable synthesis with findings, disagreements, uncertainty, and implications.

State evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, and missing capability separately when they affect the result. Stop or defer when a decision-reversing unknown cannot be resolved safely.

## Boundary

Do not calculate a pooled effect or causal conclusion unless the evidence and method justify it.

This skill is advisory expertise. MOA owns orchestration, Minister owns strategic judgment, Forge owns engineering implementation, and verification owns completion evidence.

## Evidence basis

Use the relevant parts of the [primary guidance](https://www.prisma-statement.org/prisma-2020) when standards or formal evidence are material. Verify currency before making a time-sensitive claim.
